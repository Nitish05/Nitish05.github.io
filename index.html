About Me

Hello! I'm Nitish

I build production robotics software across ROS 2 manipulation, real-time CAN actuation, perception, and control. Recent work spans MoveIt 2 arms, custom C++17 actuator SDKs with Python bindings, teleoperation systems, and embedded autonomy pipelines.

I completed my M.Eng in Robotics at the University of Maryland and now work on robot systems that connect low-level hardware, planning stacks, operator interfaces, and ML/perception models into deployable workflows.
